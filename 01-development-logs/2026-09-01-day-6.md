**Built:**

**Learned:**
Eager loading is about *loading strategy*, not query optimization. `with()` fetches relationships upfront; `withCount()` counts without loading. The real insight: an index won't fix an N+1 problem because the bottleneck is *quantity of queries*, not speed of individual queries. Eager loading reduces from 251 to 2 queries; indexing helps those 2 run faster, but it's a secondary optimization.
