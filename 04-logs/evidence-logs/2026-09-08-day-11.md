## 3. Summary

| # | Check | Expected | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `GET /me` with no token | 401 envelope | pass |
| 2 | `GET /plans/{id}` with no token | 401 envelope | pass |
| 3 | `GET /me` with a malformed token | 401 envelope | pass |
| 4 | Login, wrong password | 401 `invalid_credentials` | pass |
| 5 | Login, unknown email | **identical** message | pass — no account enumeration |
| 6 | `GET /me` as member | 200, role + own plan id | pass |
| 7 | `POST /register` asking for `role=administrator` | 201 with `role=member` | pass — escalation refused |
| 8 | Member reads own plan | 200 | pass |
| 9 | Member reads another member's plan | 403 envelope | pass |
| 10 | Administrator reads the same plan | 200 | pass |
| 11 | Read a plan that does not exist | 404 envelope, not 500 | pass |
| 12 | Member scores their own plan | 403 | pass |
| 13 | Member scores another plan | 403 | pass |
| 14 | Administrator scores a member's plan | 201 | pass |
| 15 | **Administrator scores their OWN plan** | **403** | **pass — the integrity rule** |
| 16 | Score of 150 submitted | 422 with field detail | pass |
| 17 | Revoked token reused after logout | 401 | pass |
| 18 | Six failed logins on one account | 401 ×5 then 429 | pass |
| 19 | A different account from the same IP | 200 | pass — the office is not locked out |

19 of 19 behaved as designed.

Checks 15 and 19 were the two worth running. Check 15 is the rule the tool's credibility rests on, exercised against a full administrator holding a plan of their own. Check 19 proves the login limiter confines a lockout to the account under attack rather than to everyone sharing the office IP address.

## 4. Note on the first run

The first pass of check 18 returned 429 on every attempt, because the earlier login checks in section 2 had already consumed the IP-keyed quota. The behaviour was correct and the evidence was worthless — it never showed the transition from allowed to throttled. Rerun after the limiter was rekeyed on email + IP, which is what produced the 401 ×5 → 429 sequence above.
