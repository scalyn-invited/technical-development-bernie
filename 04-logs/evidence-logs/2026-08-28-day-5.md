## Day 5 Evidence — Eloquent relationships and validation

### Models & Relationships Implemented
- **Player**: belongsTo(Team) · belongsToMany(MatchGame with pivot: position, status, timestamps) · hasManyThrough(Venue)
- **Team**: hasMany(Player)
- **MatchGame**: belongsTo(Venue) · belongsToMany(Player with pivot: position, status, timestamps)
- **Venue**: hasMany(MatchGame)

### Pivot Table Migration
- Table: `match_game_player`
- Columns: id, match_game_id (FK→cascadeOnDelete), player_id (FK→cascadeOnDelete), position (nullable string), status (default: 'pending'), timestamps
- Status: migrated and seeded

### Form Request Validation (StorePlayerRequest)
- `required` rule: name (string, max 255), email, team_id
- `exists` rule: team_id exists in teams table
- `unique` rule: email (ignore current player on update), jersey_number (ignore current player on update)
- Custom rule: phone field must be ≥10 digits (validated in closure)
