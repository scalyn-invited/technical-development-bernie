## 4. Constraints proven by making them fire

```
=== CONSTRAINTS ===
PASS: unique(plan, skill, type) rejected a duplicate baseline
PASS: restrictOnDelete blocked deletion of an assessed skill
PASS: cascadeOnDelete removed the member's plan (0) and its 3 assessments (0 remain)
```

Each of the three was an attempt to do the forbidden thing against the running application, not an assertion that it would be forbidden. The database was reset with `migrate:fresh --seed` afterwards.
